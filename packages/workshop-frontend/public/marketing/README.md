# Marketing product evidence

These assets show a real localized azhen Workshop. They are product demonstrations, not customer
case studies.

- `product-evidence-en.jpg` is the English Workshop and result state.
- `product-evidence-zh.jpg` is the equivalent Chinese Workshop and result state.
- `product-evidence-en-mobile.jpg` and `product-evidence-zh-mobile.jpg` are matching 360 × 400
  detail crops for narrow landing-page layouts. They focus on the first identified hook and the
  reasons it works, without resampling the complete Workspace capture to mobile width.
- The complete images were captured from the same local Marketing Demo Workspace at 1440 × 900
  in the light theme, with the same Gadget, composition, and saved editable result. The mobile
  images are direct crops of those captures.
- The fictional brand is **Morrow Tea / 暮茶**. The benchmark set contains 12 synthetic
  Xiaohongshu posts. No real post, creator, customer, account, credential, or personal data is
  present.
- The result identifies three content hooks and includes a real editable text field. Editing and
  the local save confirmation were verified before capture.
- The images themselves label the data as fictional and state that no third-party endorsement is
  implied. Keep that treatment when the Marketing Landing Page uses them.

Use `/marketing/product-evidence-en.jpg` for English and
`/marketing/product-evidence-zh.jpg` for Chinese. Do not describe either image as a customer story
or testimonial. At viewport widths up to 640 CSS pixels, show the locale-matched `-mobile.jpg`
asset at its intrinsic 360 CSS-pixel width instead of shrinking the complete Workspace capture.
